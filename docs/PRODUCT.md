# Desk

**Tagline:** One profile. One description. Nothing is sent.
**Name:** Desk

Private studio for Aryan Jaiswal. It writes a one-page resume and a five-paragraph cover letter from a job description, and it can shortlist public roles. He downloads the files and submits them himself.

## Who this is for

One person. There is no visitor site and no second account.

## Surfaces

1. **Desk** on this Mac, and a Vercel copy locked to his Vercel login.

## Pages

Home, Tailor, Projects, Shortlist, Profile, Log.

## Layers

1. Profile and one-page tailor. Done.
2. Cover letter in the same five-paragraph shape. Done.
3. Pin two projects. Done.
4. Public-board shortlist that stops before submit. Done.
5. Form pre-fill on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and Workday. Not built. The shortlist opens the posting instead.

## Rules that do not move

- Every number and tool comes from `lib/profile.ts`.
- The resume and the letter are one page.
- Action verbs are not repeated on a single resume.
- LinkedIn is not automated.
- A generated file is not an application until he sends it.
